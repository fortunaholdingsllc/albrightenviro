Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details regarding environmental due diligence standards, qualifications, and inspection protocols.

An Environmental Professional (EP) meets the explicit education, training, and professional experience requirements set forth in 40 CFR § 312.10. An EP possesses the necessary expertise to conduct All Appropriate Inquiries (AAI) and evaluate recognized environmental conditions (RECs).
A Record Search with Risk Assessment (RSRA) is a desktop-only review combining regulatory database checks and historical research without a physical site walk, commonly used for lower-risk properties under SBA loan guidelines. A Phase I ESA (ASTM E1527-21) includes a full physical site reconnaissance, interviews, user questionnaires, and comprehensive historical analysis.
During an E&S site visit ($250/visit), active Best Management Practices (BMPs) such as silt fencing, inlet protection, construction entrances, and sediment basins are physically inspected for integrity and compliance with local/state runoff permits. An inspection report and photo log are generated.
Post-Construction Stormwater Management (PCSM) inspections ($250/visit) evaluate permanent detention/retention basins, underground detention vaults, outflow control structures, and vegetated channels for structural stability, sediment accumulation, and functional discharge operation.
